\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In Singapore, the real estate market is differentiated into the public sector (regulated by the Housing \& Development Board) and the private sector. Unfortunately, the information available from the four competing real estate portals is fragmented and without any unified structure. This report outlines the process and methodology to develop and establish a system for integrating the information from these four portals, including data scanning, deduplication and ranking, as well as search capabilities.

The system was built by a team and has strong web scraping pipelines that collect live data from big property sites like PropertyGuru, 99.co, EdgeProp, and SRX. The author personally designed and implemented the PropertyGuru scraper—the largest data source comprising over 53,000 listings—as well as the centralized aggregation and deduplication pipeline. Our algorithm for deduplicating composite keys uses a title-fallback method. It covers 95.5\% of address fields and works well with a variety of data structures. It also gets rid of duplicates on different platforms.

Based on this unified dataset, the author implemented advanced analytical capabilities and a user-friendly application suite. Eight Gradient Boosting Valuation (XGBoost) models targeting specific market segments exhibited a low mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) of 8.9\%. Integrating SHAP for interpretability, the models achieved a 70\% error reduction over the baseline. An ablation study further confirmed that the model's logic aligns with real-world market factors. Specifically, removing \texttt{property\_age} resulted in a 130\,pp increase in MAPE for HDB Sales, while Landed Sales only saw a 1.2\,pp rise. This difference aligns with Singapore's CPF lease-eligibility policy and has resulted in a unique demand-side discontinuity in the stock of older HDB flats. This highlights the rationality of this model's feature utilisation within its economic structure. Furthermore, the LLM-powered search engine achieved a 96.4\% F1 score in translating queries to database filters, with a blind evaluation (18 queries) confirming an adjusted F1 of $\sim$91\%. Three agentic enhancements were layered on top: multi-district geographic resolution, progressive filter relaxation with zero-result prevention, and LLM-generated fallback explanations. A multi-turn chat assistant rounds out the UI, providing property valuations driven by XGBoost and SHAP importance factors. Across three different segments of real estate, 42 interactions were analyzed offline and it was found that 93\% of the facts provided during these 42 interactions were accurate; all 100\% of the responses given during these interactions were pertinent. The decoupling architecture was developed using a FastAPI backend for serving data simultaneously to multiple users and a Next.js frontend for creating an interactive user experience, thereby transforming live raw market data into meaningful, multi-dimensional insight around real estate.
